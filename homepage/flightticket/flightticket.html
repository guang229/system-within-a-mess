.˖ Flight Ticket

I'm inching towards the time during the semester where I'm getting sick and tired of everything, and I spend copious amounts of time on the 'NYC to Anywhere' flights feature on Google. On this particular day, I found an old flight ticket from spring break last year when I went to Cancun, Mexico. For the past five years, I've kept all of my flight tickets as mementos. You call it hoarding, I call it sentimentality. But in finding this flight ticket I got to reminisce on that particular trip. What I would do to be on the beach, UV 10, and a limitless amount of Pina Colada's within reach. I wouldn't pay $300 for a flight ticket and miss class time during the semester though, so I guess I'll stick to reminiscing.
