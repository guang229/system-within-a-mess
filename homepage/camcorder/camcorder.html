.˖ My Camcorder

I always told myself I could get by with my phone, but I could never shake the feeling that those shaky, overexposed clips were selling my moments short. Recently, though, I finally got my hands on a little camcorder that feels almost embarrassingly official, and I’ve been treating it like a fragile newborn ever since. It reminds me of the bulky recorder my dad used to haul out for every birthday and holiday, the one that made us all sit a little straighter when the red light blinked on. This one is sleeker, lighter, and maybe a bit too high-tech for someone who still fumbles with the settings menu, but every time I hear the quiet whir of it starting up, I feel like I’m back in those living-room scenes, except now I’m the one behind the lens.
