Stay with me now. Since I can remember, my father would always play sudoku on his phone. I was always so intrigued by it, but my dad would straight up tell me that it was too complex for me. I loved proving peoples expectations to be wrong, and that's what I did. Sudoku was one of the first apps I downloaded when I got my first phone at 12, and I have beaten my father's record speed on every single level, outscored him by hundreds of points, and I brag about this only because I've spent so much time playing sudoku that if I don't I fear it would be for nothing. Beyond defying my father's expectations, I have grown to truly love sudoku. I've always enjoyed solving puzzles, but nothing tickles my brain like this has. Anyways, when I was in the airport during spring break last semester, I realised I couldn't use my phone much as it would've ran out of battery. I went to the bookstore and settled for this sudoku booklet, of all things in that store.
