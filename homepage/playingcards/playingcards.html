A deck of cards

Last spring semester, one of my roommates, Pema, and I developed a truly all-consuming obsession with card games. We would sit in the park, or on our dorm's Twin XL's, playing our card games through rain and shine. It was to the point where we have submitted assignments late, contemplated skipping class, and ruined our sleep schedules, just so we could play a round of 'Spit'. Unfortunately, as sophomores in fashion and communication design majors, we have not been given the same luxury of time that we had previously. However, thankfully for the both of us, we were able to satiate our craving by spending time playing card games when she visited my apartment over the weekend.
