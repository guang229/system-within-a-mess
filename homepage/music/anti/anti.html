What I'm listening to

Music is a really big part of my life, I can say this with confidence as my Spotify wrapped showed that I've listened to 61,490 minutes of music last year. This being said, the music I listen to on a particular day, week, month, tends to be very reflective of what I'm feeling, and it helps me navigate my emotions better. I was feeling inexplicably devious this week. I wish I could elaborate but there really is no other way to describe my attitude this week. Maybe it was the fact that I had to get my puffer out of storage, or that I missed TWO opportunities to see Daniel Caesar (my favourite artist) live, but the resentment and female rage Rihanna captured in this album was the only way to soundtrack this week. Other than that though, I really just miss Rihanna, where is her next album. My favourite songs include Consideration, Kiss it Better, and Love on the Brain.
